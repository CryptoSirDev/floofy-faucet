❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FloofyChain Faucet?

The official FloofyChain faucet is a free tool that allows users to claim free FLOOF crypto tokens daily to test and explore the network.

How often can I claim FLOOF tokens?

You can submit your wallet address to claim free tokens once every 24 hours per IP address and wallet address.

How do I check my transaction (TXID)?

After a successful claim, a clickable transaction hash (TXID) will appear. You can click it to instantly open and verify your transaction on the official FloofyChain Explorer.

How can I support the Faucet?

You can help keep the reward pool filled by donating FLOOF tokens directly to the community donation address.
