❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FloofyChain Faucet?

The official FloofyChain faucet is a free online tool that allows users and developers to claim free FLOOF crypto tokens daily to test transactions, deploy smart contracts, and explore the network.

How often can I claim free FLOOF tokens?

You can submit your wallet address to claim free tokens once every 24 hours per IP address and wallet address to prevent spam and ensure fair distribution.

Are there any fees to use the Floofy Faucet?

No, claiming tokens from the official FloofyChain faucet is completely free. You only need to provide your public wallet address to receive the test tokens.

How do I check my transaction (TXID)?

After a successful claim, a clickable transaction hash (TXID) will appear. You can click it to instantly open and verify your transaction on the official FloofyChain Explorer.

What can I do with FLOOF tokens?

FLOOF tokens can be used within the FloofyChain ecosystem to test decentralized applications, cover network fees, and explore blockchain features.

How can I support and donate to the Faucet?

You can help keep the community reward pool filled by donating FLOOF tokens directly to the official community donation address listed on the faucet.
